# The Heavenly Pattern

*The things God told Moses to make, and what they copied*

"And let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell in their midst. Exactly as I show you concerning the
pattern of the tabernacle, and of all its furniture, so you shall make it" (Exodus 25:8-9, ESV).

This series takes the furnishings one at a time. Each page follows its furnishing from Sinai through
Solomon's temple, the prophets and the New Testament, and ends with what Scripture says the heavenly
original is. Each has a plate drawn from what the text states, with anything the text leaves unstated
drawn dashed and its source named.

Each page sorts its connections to Christ by how directly Scripture makes them: stated in the New
Testament, echoed there, or read in by later interpreters. Hebrews itself sets the limit, stopping
after the list of furnishings: "Of these things we cannot now speak in detail" (Hebrews 9:5, ESV).

## The furnishings

- [The Ark of the Covenant](ark.md) — the gold chest under the mercy seat, lost on earth, named in
  Jesus as the propitiation, and seen in the temple in heaven.
- [The Golden Altar of Incense](incense-altar.md) — the altar before the veil where incense rose with
  the lamps, and the altar before the throne where the saints' prayers rise.
- [The Lampstand](lampstand.md) — one almond tree of hammered gold, kept burning every night, and the
  seven lampstands Jesus walks among in Revelation.
