# Sin

Sin, temptation, and the pattern of redemption in Scripture.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   __Idolatry__

    ---

    Idolatry is giving a created thing the trust and service that belong to God. The made god is nothing, but worship offered to it goes to demons. Paul names greed as idolatry in the church, and pride is the self-sufficiency that opens the door to both. God answers with a jealous love and the call back to His Son Jesus Christ, the true God.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](idolatry.md)

-   __Pride__

    ---

    Pride is self-sufficiency: living as though you need no one above you, and at its root wanting God's place. It comes out of the heart, it deceives the one who has it, and God sets Himself against it. The way down is to humble yourself and boast in the cross of Jesus.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](pride.md)

-   __Sin and Sorcery__

    ---

    Why Scripture forbids sorcery: an exegetical study of Deuteronomy 18:9-22, where the fullest list of occult practices in the Bible sits immediately before the promise of the Prophet God would raise up.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](sorcery.md)

-   __Why We Need Saving__

    ---

    God is good, and He gave His commands for your good. Sin is turning from His way to your own, believing the lie that His good is evil and your way is better. Since Adam everyone has done it, so sin separates you from God, enslaves you and earns you death. That is why God sent His Son Jesus to die for you while you were still a sinner.
