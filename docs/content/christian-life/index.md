-   __Fasting__

    ---

    Fasting in Scripture is humbling yourself before God: going without food to seek Him in repentance, grief, need and decision, while the Bridegroom is away. What the Bible shows fasting doing, what it never promises, and the verses most often misused about it.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](fasting.md)

-   __Forgive Us Our Debts__

    ---

    God forgives by cancelling a debt you could never pay, and the forgiveness is received by everyone who repents. A study of Matthew 6:14-15: what biblical forgiveness is, how it differs from love and from trust, what to do when the one who wronged you keeps on, and how to forgive without false guilt.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](forgiveness.md)

-   __How God Answers Prayer__

    ---

    Heard at once, answered by varied routes and on God's own timetable, sometimes missed while it happens, and kept: what Scripture shows about the answers to prayer.

-   __In Humility__

    ---

    Humility is living before God as someone who depends on Him for everything, as Jesus lived before His Father all the way to the cross. It shows itself in counting others more significant than yourself, and God lifts it up. A study of Philippians 2:1-11.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](humility.md)

-   __Hurt by the Church: Wolves, Weeds and the Good Shepherd__

    ---

    People in church get hurt by the people they trusted. Scripture names four kinds of hurt: brothers who fail you, shepherds who harm, wolves who prey, and the weeds of the world. It answers each one differently, and it points every one of them to the Good Shepherd who stays.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](hurt-by-the-church.md)

-   __Know the Truth__

    ---

    Truth is God's own firmness, made flesh in His Son Jesus. You know it by abiding in His word, and it frees you from sin and from the father of lies. A study of John 8:31-47.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](know-the-truth.md)

-   __The Lord's Prayer: What Jesus Taught Us About How to Pray__

    ---

    Line by line through the prayer Jesus gave His disciples in Matthew 6:9-13 and Luke 11:2-4 -- what each petition asks, the words and background behind it, and how to pray it today.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](lords-prayer.md)

    ---

    The virtues of 2 Peter 1:3-11 as the fruit of a nature God has given: everything for life and godliness already granted, real effort that is never a burden, the joy that comes with it, and a rich entrance into Christ's kingdom.

    ---

    Jesus invites the weary to Himself and gives them rest under His yoke. Hebrews tells tired believers to run with endurance, looking to Him. Why Christians grow weary, what feeds the runner, and what weighs the runner down.

    ---

    Jesus told His disciples, "You are the salt of the earth" and "You are the light of the world"; Paul wrote, "we are the aroma of Christ to God." What each picture meant to its first hearers, what it asks of one believer and of a whole church, and the churches Jesus calls lampstands.

A study of Matthew 16:21-28 through two Greek word studies: the mind that keeps itself safe and high, the life saved by losing it, and Peter's own road from that rebuke to his denial, his tears and his restoration.

-   __Test the Spirits: Recognising False Teachers and Wolves__

    ---

    The apostles expected false teachers to rise inside the church. Scripture tells you to keep testing every teacher by three marks: the Christ they preach, the life they live, and where they lead you. Signs, crowds and Bible words prove nothing, and the One in you is greater.

-   __Those Who Sigh and Groan: A Heart After God's Own__

    ---

    In Ezekiel 9 God sent a scribe through Jerusalem to mark everyone who sighed and groaned over its abominations, and the marked were spared. What grieves God grieves His own, and in Christ He gives that heart and seals it with His Spirit.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](those-who-sigh-and-groan.md)

-   __True Worship__

    ---

    Worship is bowing your whole self before God because He is worthy as Creator and Redeemer. Before Jesus it came through a place, a priesthood and sacrifice; since Jesus it comes through Him, by the Holy Spirit and in truth, with your whole life; in the kingdom and the new creation every nation and creature will give it. It belongs to God alone: angels refuse it, the beast demands it, and Jesus receives it.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Read](true-worship.md)

    ---

    Jesus said the love of the many would grow cold. For the believer watching a friend's faith fade, Scripture adds Elijah's seven thousand, Isaiah's remnant, the disciples who fell away and were called back, and the Lord who knows those who are His.
