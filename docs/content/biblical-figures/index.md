# Biblical Figures

Studies of particular people in Scripture — the twelve disciples, the patriarchs, the prophets,
the kings.

Filing a study here is a decision about what it is *most* about. A study of Melchizedek's
priesthood is [Christology](../jesus/melchizedek-priesthood.md), because its subject is the office
Christ holds rather than the man who briefly held it first. A study belongs here when the person is
the subject, not the illustration.
