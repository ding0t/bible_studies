# Chronology

Dating biblical history: what can be fixed to a year, how the numbers in Scripture add up, and
the one timeline this site works to. Every date on the
[Prophetic Timeline](../timeline.md) is argued for here, and its
[Chart view](/timeline/#view=chart) prints any stretch of it on A4 sheets.

The studies read in order. They start from the events an outside record fixes, Solomon's temple
onward, and count back from there through the kings, the Exodus and the genealogies of Genesis 5
and 11, ending with the whole line from creation to the present.

Other studies use these dates for their own subjects:

- [Prophecy Events and Times](../last-things/prophecy-events-times.md),
  [Six Days of History](../last-things/six-days-of-history.md) and
  [A Day Is a Thousand Years](../last-things/day-is-a-thousand-years.md) read the timeline as a
  week of thousand-year days.
- [The Zadok Calendar](../feasts/zadok-calendar.md) is the 364-day priestly calendar behind the
  site's Zadok years.
- [Firstfruits](../feasts/firstfruits.md) and
  [Three Days and Three Nights](../jesus/three-days-and-three-nights.md) build on the crucifixion
  year settled in Chronology Anchors.
- [What World Population Declares](world-population-declares-gods-creation-and-biblical-truth.md)
  checks the creation date against the arithmetic of population growth.
