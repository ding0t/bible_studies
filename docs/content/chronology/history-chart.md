# History Chart

God works His purpose out in history, and He tells us its shape: "with the Lord one day is as a
thousand years, and a thousand years as one day" (2 Peter 3:8, ESV). This chart lays that history
out on paper, to print, pin up and teach from. Each of the seven days of the creation week stands
over its thousand years, and the ages run across the top in the names Christians have long used
for them: before the Flood, after the Flood, the patriarchs, Israel under the Law, the church age,
the millennial kingdom and the new creation.

Under them run the lanes. **Scripture and history** carries the events with a picture over the
main ones. **People** draws each life in the genealogy from Adam to Jesus to scale, so you can see
Methuselah die in the year of the Flood and Shem outlive Abraham's birth by centuries. **Texts and
finds** places the manuscripts, inscriptions and ancient writers from
[Ancient Texts and Manuscripts](../scripture/ancient-texts-manuscripts.md) against the history
they bear witness to. **Threads** follow a promise to its fulfilment: the promised Seed from
Abraham to the cross, the temple from Solomon to the Passover in John 2, and Daniel's sixty-nine
weeks.

Every date comes from the same data as the [Prophetic Timeline](../timeline.md) and is argued for
in the [Chronology](index.md) studies. Anno Mundi (years since creation) runs along the top and
BC/AD along the bottom.

Pick a period, or type any range of years, and choose how many A4 sheets to spread it across.
**Scale fitted to events** gives crowded centuries more room and marks each change of scale with
a fold on the axis; **Even scale** keeps one scale throughout and says how long the whole week
would be at it. Every lane can be switched off, and **Black and white** redraws the chart for a
mono printer, with the ages told apart by pattern. Print landscape at 100% with no margins, or
download a sheet as SVG for a print shop. Click anything on a sheet for its dates, the verses
behind it and the study that treats it.
