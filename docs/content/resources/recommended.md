# Recommended Bible Resources

Sites, tools and channels I use alongside these studies. Test them the same way you test these
notes, against Scripture (Acts 17:11).

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-frequently-asked-questions:{ .lg .middle } __Got Questions__

    ---

    Little snippets of well-structured expositional answers. Type a question the way you would ask
    it ("Who were the Nephilim?", "What is propitiation?") and you get a short, Scripture-referenced
    answer from a conservative evangelical, nondenominational ministry running since 2002. It is the
    quickest way to get oriented on a topic before going deeper, and each answer points to the
    passages that matter, which are the passages to read next.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: gotquestions.org](https://www.gotquestions.org/)

-   :material-book-search:{ .lg .middle } __Blue Letter Bible__

    ---

    Free online bible study tooling. Its interlinear lines up every word of the Hebrew and Greek with
    its Strong's number, lexicon entry and every other place it occurs, across dozens of English
    translations, with classic commentaries alongside. It is what lets a reader without the
    original languages do a word study. This site's own pop-ups show a verse and its word in place;
    Blue Letter Bible is where to follow a word all the way through.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: blueletterbible.org](https://www.blueletterbible.org/)

-   :material-book-open-variant:{ .lg .middle } __Bible Facts__

    ---

    Excellent studies based in biblical and extra-biblical texts. Ken Johnson's ministry works from
    the ancient sources themselves: the church fathers, the Dead Sea Scrolls and other early
    documents, to show how the earliest believers read Scripture. It carries live-streamed teaching,
    prophecy charts and study files. Read it for the ancient witness on a question, and check what
    you find there against the primary text, as with any source.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: biblefacts.org](https://biblefacts.org/)

-   :material-shovel:{ .lg .middle } __Expedition Bible__

    ---

    Joel Kramer, an archaeologist with a master's in biblical archaeology who has excavated in
    Jerusalem, Bethlehem and Ai, takes you to the biblical sites on location and shows the
    evidence in the ground. His book *Where God Came Down* works through ten of them. Seeing the
    place makes the text concrete: the distances, the terrain and the layers of a dig show why a
    passage reads the way it does, and they show the Bible's history standing up to the spade.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: expeditionbible.com](https://www.expeditionbible.com/) ·
    [:material-youtube: YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/ExpeditionBible)

-   :material-earth:{ .lg .middle } __eBible.org__

    ---

    Free Bibles in many languages and formats; also the source behind several translations this
    site's own tools query directly. Its texts are public domain or openly licensed, so they can be
    downloaded, printed, quoted at length and shared without permission. That includes the World
    English Bible this site's pop-ups show and the Greek Septuagint its tools search. It is the place to get
    Scripture for a language, a device or a project of your own.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: ebible.org](https://ebible.org/)

</div>
