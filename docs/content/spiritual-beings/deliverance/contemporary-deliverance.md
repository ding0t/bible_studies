# Contemporary deliverance in Christianity

This page collects what contemporary deliverance ministries teach, in their own words.

Its subject is one claim: that a Christian can be "demonized." Casting a demon out of someone who
does not have the Holy Spirit is not in dispute. "Demonized" translates the same Greek verb as
"demon-possessed" (see the [word study in Christians and Deliverance Ministry](christians-and-deliverance.md#the-key-term-demonized)),
so the milder-sounding word carries the same claim.

Your time is better spent reading God's Word. This survey is for anyone already influenced by one of
these ministries.

**In one sentence:** Every ministry surveyed here offers more than Christ has given, and the texts behind its vocabulary, read in context, show that God has already cancelled your debt at the cross, disarmed the powers and filled you in Christ, so you need nothing added to Him.

## What the ministries share

The same pattern recurs in every ministry below:

- **"More" from God.** Each offers something beyond what is already accomplished in Christ.
- **A technical vocabulary.** "Demonized," "soul ties," "authority" and "sin energy" turn ordinary
  Christian categories (sin, forgiveness, temptation) into problems a session or a practitioner must
  resolve.

[The vocabulary, weighed](#the-vocabulary-weighed), after the ministries, reads the text behind each
term in its context. [Christians and Deliverance Ministry](christians-and-deliverance.md) gives the
biblical case against a believer being "demonized," word study included.

<https://www.liberatedliving.info/>

God-given right to be there.

demons. The "right to be there" is weighed [below](#a-god-given-right-to-be-there).

## The vocabulary, weighed

Each term the ministries use has a text behind it, or near it. Here is what each text says in its
context.

### "Soul ties"

Scripture does speak of souls bound together. "The soul of Jonathan was knit to the soul of David"
(1 Samuel 18:1, ESV). "Was knit" is <span dir="rtl">נִקְשְׁרָה</span> (*niqšerāh*, nik-sheh-RAH),
from <span dir="rtl">קָשַׁר</span> (*qāšar*, H7194), "to bind". Two verses later Jonathan makes a
covenant with David (1 Samuel 18:3), and Scripture commends the friendship. Sexual union joins too:
"he who is joined to a prostitute becomes one body with her" (1 Corinthians 6:16, ESV). Paul's
remedy is "Flee from sexual immorality" (1 Corinthians 6:18, ESV), set beside a greater union: "he
who is joined to the Lord becomes one spirit with him" (1 Corinthians 6:17, ESV). Neither text
describes a spiritual channel that a prayer must sever.

### "Generational curses"

The text behind the term is Exodus 20:5. God visits "the iniquity of the fathers on the children to
the third and the fourth generation of those who hate me" (ESV). The verse names the generations it
means: "those who hate me". The next verse sets the scale beside it: "steadfast love to thousands of
those who love me" (Exodus 20:6, ESV). When the exiles turned the idea into a proverb about sour
grapes (Ezekiel 18:2), God answered, "The son shall not suffer for the iniquity of the father"
(Ezekiel 18:20, ESV). And the curse has a named end: "Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law by
becoming a curse for us" (Galatians 3:13, ESV).

### "Word curses"

"Like a sparrow in its flitting, like a swallow in its flying, a curse that is causeless does not
alight" (Proverbs 26:2, ESV). Balak hired Balaam to curse Israel, and complained, "you have done
nothing but bless them" (Numbers 23:11, ESV). Balaam's own oracle gives the reason: "there is no
enchantment against Jacob, no divination against Israel" (Numbers 23:23, ESV). God protects His
people from the curse spoken against them.

### "A God-given right to be there"

Dickerman's phrase is a legal one, and Paul answers it in legal language. God forgave "all our
trespasses, by canceling the record of debt that stood against us with its legal demands. This he
set aside, nailing it to the cross. He disarmed the rulers and authorities and put them to open
shame, by triumphing over them in him" (Colossians 2:13-15, ESV). The only claim against you was the
record of your debt. God nailed it to the cross, and in the next sentence the powers are disarmed.

### "Authority is developmental"

"Behold, I have given you authority to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of
the enemy" (Luke 10:19, ESV). "I have given" is δέδωκα (*dedōka*), the perfect of δίδωμι (*didōmi*,
DID-oh-mee, G1325): given, and still in force. Jesus then turned the disciples' joy away from it: "do
not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you, but rejoice that your names are written in
heaven" (Luke 10:20, ESV). The authority is His gift and rests on Him: "All authority in heaven and
on earth has been given to me" (Matthew 28:18, ESV).

### More than Christ has given

"For in him the whole fullness of deity dwells bodily, and you have been filled in him, who is the
head of all rule and authority" (Colossians 2:9-10, ESV). "You have been filled" is the perfect
participle πεπληρωμένοι (*peplērōmenoi*): filled, and still full. Paul wrote it to a church being
offered something beyond Christ: "See to it that no one takes you captive by philosophy and empty
deceit... and not according to Christ" (Colossians 2:8, ESV).
