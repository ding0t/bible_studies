# Sermon principles

How to write one

## Principles

1. Pray - lead in prayer at all points
1. Teach bible based truth
1. Provide scripture references as the basis
1. Point to Christ
1. Do not be afraid to challenge contemporary lies that are against the truth - do not apologise
1. Be clear. Have a clear focus to the teaching
1. Your style is more teaching than inspirational sermon
