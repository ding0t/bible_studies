# The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is God, the third person of the Trinity, equal with the Father and the Son. He is a
person: He teaches and reminds believers of what Jesus said (John 14:26), convicts the world
concerning sin, righteousness and judgment (John 16:8), and seals every believer as the guarantee of
what is to come (Ephesians 1:13-14). This section sits beside [God the Father](../god/index.md) and
[Jesus](../jesus/index.md), God the Son, because the three persons are one God. The
[Statement of Faith](../about/statement-of-faith.md#god-the-holy-spirit) sets out what this site
believes about Him.
