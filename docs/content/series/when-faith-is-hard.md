# When Faith Is Hard

**In one sentence:** Jesus invites the weary to Himself and gives them rest, stays when His people
hurt you, hears you from your first word and forgives your whole debt, so you can keep going with
Him when faith is hard.

A series for a group going through something, or for one person walking with another. The weeks
move from rest, through the hurts that wear faith down, to the grief that God shares.
