# The Gospel in the Old Testament

**In one sentence:** God drew the gospel into Israel's history before He sent it, in a lamb's blood
on the door, a serpent lifted on a pole, blood behind the veil, a kinsman who buys back and a priest
who lives forever, so you can see in His Son Jesus what He had been promising all along.

On the road to Emmaus Jesus began with Moses and all the Prophets and interpreted to the two
disciples the things concerning Himself (Luke 24:27). Each week takes one of those pictures, and
each study sorts what the New Testament states about it from what later readers have added.
