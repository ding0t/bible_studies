# The Olivet Discourse, Week by Week

**In one sentence:** Jesus refused His disciples a checklist and named one sign, His own return; His
words will outlast heaven and earth and the day is the Father's to know, so you are to stay awake and
ready for Him.

Week one reads the whole discourse; the rest take Matthew 24:29-41 a few verses at a time, in order.
