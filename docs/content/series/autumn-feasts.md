# The Autumn Feasts

**In one sentence:** God has kept the Day of Atonement once for all in His Son Jesus (Hebrews 9), and
the trumpet and the booths look ahead to the day He is king over all the earth and dwells with His
people (Zechariah 14:16-19), so you can keep these weeks as someone who has been made clean and is
waiting for Him.

The feasts fall in the seventh month of Israel's calendar, September to October, in the order of
Leviticus 23.
