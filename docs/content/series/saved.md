# Saved: Salvation Foundations

**In one sentence:** God saves you by His grace through faith in His Son Jesus (Ephesians 2:8-9):
He declares you righteous, adopts you as His child, makes you holy by His Spirit and keeps you, so
you can know you are His.

The weeks follow the order of Ephesians 2:1-10: dead in sin, made alive by grace, saved through
faith, and created in Christ Jesus for good works. The weeks marked *coming* are being written; the
series can be run now with the published weeks, or in full once they are.
