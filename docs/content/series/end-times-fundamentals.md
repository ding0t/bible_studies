# End Times Fundamentals

**In one sentence:** God has already kept prophecies anyone can check, and the same God has told you
how history ends, with His Son Jesus coming for His Church and reigning as King, so you can wait for
Him with comfort and spend the waiting at work.

The order runs from method to hope to practice. Week one sets out how prophecy is read; weeks two and
three give the frame of the kingdom and of Daniel's history; weeks four to six take the promises made
to the Church; week seven turns them into how to live while you wait.
