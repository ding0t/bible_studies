# God the Father

There is one God, eternally existing in three persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit, each
fully God. Moses heard "The LORD our God, the LORD is one" (Deuteronomy 6:4, ESV), and Jesus sent His
disciples to baptize "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit"
(Matthew 28:19, ESV). This site gives the three persons three sections side by side: this one, [Jesus](../jesus/index.md),
God the Son, and [The Holy Spirit](../holy-spirit/index.md).

This section studies the Father, "from whom are all things and for whom we exist"
(1 Corinthians 8:6, ESV), together with what is true of the one God: His names, His nature, creation, and
the ways He makes Himself known. The [Statement of Faith](../about/statement-of-faith.md#one-god-in-three)
sets out what this site believes about the Trinity.
